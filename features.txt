==============================================================
                     JB HUB  -  FEATURES
                  Jailbreak script for Matcha
                       made by uhq7k
==============================================================

  [H] = requires Matcha Hybrid Mode


--------------------------------------------------------------
  MISC
--------------------------------------------------------------
  No Fall Damage ........... Take no damage from falls (2 methods)
  No Laser Damage .......... Lasers in robberies no longer hurt you
  No Turret Damage ......... Disables Military, Oil Rig, Cargo Ship
                             and Home turrets
  Disable Forcefields ...... Walk through the Military Base dome and
                             ignore the Mansion CEO aura blast
  Anti Taser Ragdoll ....... Get back up instantly when tased
  Unlock Weapons Slots ..... Switch weapons while holding a bag
  Infinity Jetpack ......... Unlimited jetpack fuel
  Auto-Solve Puzzle ........ Solves the Power Plant puzzle on a key
  Show Puzzle Path ......... Draws the solution path on screen
  Force Reset .............. Instantly respawns your character


--------------------------------------------------------------
  ESP
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Airdrop ESP .............. Shows airdrops with their color
  Keycard ESP .............. Shows keycards on the map
  Crimekey ESP ............. Shows crime keys on the map
  Cash Dropped ESP ......... Shows dropped cash and its amount
  Bounty ESP ............... Shows players with a bounty
                             (custom color and minimum bounty)


--------------------------------------------------------------
  CASINO  /  TOMB
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Get Casino Code [H] ...... Reads the vault code
  Open Casino Door [H] ..... Opens the casino door
  Auto Glass [H] ........... Breaks the glass automatically
  Auto Vault [H] ........... Opens the vault automatically
  Auto Collect Loot [H] .... Collects the loot automatically

  No Spike Damage .......... Tomb spikes no longer hurt you
  No Dart Damage ........... Tomb darts no longer hurt you
  Safe Bridge .............. The Tomb bridge no longer collapses
  No Cart Barrier Damage ... Cart barriers no longer hurt you


--------------------------------------------------------------
  MODIFIERS
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Sword No Cooldown ........ Swing the sword without delay
  Sword Always Lunge ....... Every swing is a lunge
  Punch No Cooldown ........ Punch without delay
  Instant Interaction ...... Doors, buttons and more open instantly
  Instant Plasma Pistol .... Empties the whole magazine at once
  Rocket Instant Lock ...... Rocket Launcher locks on instantly
  No Recoil ................ Removes weapon recoil
  No Spread ................ Removes bullet spread
  Auto Shoot ............... Makes every weapon fully automatic


--------------------------------------------------------------
  WEAPONS
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Nerf Skins ............... Pistol and Revolver use Nerf models
  Skin Changer ............. Apply any weapon skin (client-side)
  Hit Sound ................ Custom sound when you hit a player


--------------------------------------------------------------
  KILLAURA
--------------------------------------------------------------
  PlasmaPistol Killaura [H]  Auto-shoots nearby targets
                             (selectable target team)


--------------------------------------------------------------
  NPC
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Kill All NPCs ............ Kills nearby guards and NPCs
  Make Guards Disabled ..... Guards stop attacking you
  Make Guards Attack CEO ... Guards fight the Mansion boss
  Range .................... Distance used by the NPC features


--------------------------------------------------------------
  VEHICLE
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Vehicle Fly .............. Fly with any vehicle (adjustable speed)
  Engine Boost ............. Increases vehicle speed
  Instant Brake ............ Stops your vehicle instantly
  Anti Tires Pop ........... Spikes can no longer pop your tires
  Anti Flip ................ Keeps your vehicle upright
  Infinity Nitro ........... Unlimited nitro


--------------------------------------------------------------
  POLICE
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Instant Auto Cuff ........ Arrests nearby criminals instantly
  Auto Taser ............... Tases criminals in range automatically


--------------------------------------------------------------
  COSMETICS  [H]
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Color, Texture, Horn, Rim, Spoiler, Tire Style, Tire Sticker
  Apply Selected ........... Applies your selection once
  Apply Cosmetics Bind ..... Applies your selection on a key
  Loop Cosmetics ........... Re-applies it every time you get in


--------------------------------------------------------------
  WORLD
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Sky Color ................ Changes the sky color
  Water Customizer ......... Changes water color and sea state


--------------------------------------------------------------
  CONFIG
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Save / Load .............. Save and load your settings by name
